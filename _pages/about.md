Ningyuan Liu is an undergraduate student at
[Sun Yat-sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/) and is expected to receive his
B.S. degree in Computer Science in 2028. His research interests lie in
**large language models**, **vision-language models**, and **intelligent agents**.
He has authored multiple papers published at leading AI conferences, including
**ICML**, **NeurIPS**, **AAAI**, and **ICASSP**. His work has received over
**100 citations**. In addition, he serves as a reviewer for major machine learning
venues such as **ICML**, **NeurIPS**, and **TMLR**, and has been recognized as an
**ICML Gold Reviewer**.

## Research

- **Multi-agent LLM systems.** Coordinating specialized LLM experts under cost
  and latency constraints — using Bayesian bandits to route each query to the
  right expert
  ([KABB](/publication/2025-07-01-kabb), ICML 2025).
- **Agent-driven training optimization.** Treating hyperparameter and loss
  choices as decisions for a bandit-based agent that adapts data augmentation,
  optimizers, learning rates, and losses on the fly during training of
  multi-label image classifiers
  ([MAT-Agent](/publication/2025-12-01-mat-agent), NeurIPS 2025).
- **Reliable LLMs.** Correcting hallucinations in large language models at
  inference time via dynamic neuron perturbation
  ([LLM-CAS](/publication/2026-02-01-llm-cas), AAAI 2026).
- **Robust vision-language models.** Improving out-of-distribution robustness
  of VLMs through dynamic representation optimization
  ([GTMA](/publication/2026-04-01-gtma), ICASSP 2026).

See my [Publications](/publications/) page for the full list.
